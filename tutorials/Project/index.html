It's all in your head

An accurate prediction of the future of the internet

The year is 2125, and there’s a microchip in your brain. It may sound insane, scary, and even dystopian, but it’s completely normal. Everyone has one. iPhones, Androids, and all smartphones are gone, because your microchip is your phone. That’s right, you can doomscroll, shop online, and text your friends and family all with just your mind! A lot of things have changed thanks to AMAGOONET’s new technology (yup,no more FAANG, because Amazon bought out Meta & Apple, and then joined forces with Google and Netflix to create one big company). You don’t have to carry around a physical phone so no one can see your screen, because it’s all in your head.
We finally achieved privacy! You don’t have to strain your little fingers because of scrolling. Aside from all those little perks, we also got rid of all the boring jobs! There was a huge explosion from the planet overheating, and only the rich survived by hiding in their bunkers, which means everyone is a Nepo baby and is living off their great great grandparents trust funds. Now you get to be who you want (for the most part)! And don’t even worry about being an engineer or doctor, we have AI robots now, they produce our code, take care of your family, perform surgeries, and do all that boring stuff. But who’s in charge of the robots…? Thats riiiight, more robots! Yup, we have a factory which mass produces all sorts of robots using AI, and the code is nearly indestructible. So for example, when one robot doctor is broken, we already have a robot mechanic on the way to fix him.

Back in 2025, there was concern that robots would take over creative jobs, but we still wanted to preserve our human spirit, so we made a law which prohibits AI or robots touching certain jobs. This includes (but not limited to) influencers, DJ’s, streamers, and creative directors (whatever that means). Life isn’t so peachy all the time though… Picture this… You’re the great x 3 grandson of Skrillex, so of course you have to carry on the legacy as a DJ since your last 4 grandpas were all DJ’s. You’re on the lineup for this year’s 125th anniversary Oachella, but now the head of Oachella is saying they might remove you from the festival because a video of you acting out in public a few hours ago is going viral. In the video, you yelled at your robot assistant, kicked him and threw your matcha latte at him. In your defense, you didn’t sleep last night because you were up all night working on your latest remix, then your AMAGOONET stocks dropped this morning, and then came the final straw…Your assistant got you a latte with whole milk instead of oat milk. This is way worse than last year’s scandal where your whole instagram feed was filled with allegations of you cheating on your girlfriend (they were true). You call your manager in for a meeting, begging her to help you.


“Can’t you say the video was AI?” your manager asks.

“No… my robot assistant already put out a statement on Twitter, and it’s going viral” you reply.

You telepathically screen-share the tweet to your manager’s brain.

“This is really bad. I don’t think there’s much I can do,” she sighs. “This is the fourth scandal in two years. We can’t keep saying these videos are AI-generated, do you underst—”


You feel tears forming in your eyes, you stop screensharing, you try to tune her out by watching your favorite TikToker’s hot spicy noodle mukbang challenge. It doesn’t work. You storm out, your robot driver takes you home, you try to watch some tiktoks, but all you can think about is how you lost your Oachella debut.

When you get home, your girlfriend is already yelling at you. You haven’t told her yet, but she found out from Instagram gossip pages that you’re getting dropped from the Oachella lineup.
“I can’t believe you’re so selfish, I’ve been going to Oachella since I was 14, you know how special this event is to me. This is so embarrassing, you’re lucky I have 2 million followers so I still qualify for VIP. I don’t even care about VIP, it’s just the fact that you ruined my image because you can’t control your emotions.” She yells.

You can’t even look at her. You don’t know what to say or do but she just keeps going. Now she’s talking about how she wanted to make an Oachella couples vlog with you for her Insta, but you ruined that too. Out of pure panic and habit, you open TikTok. For a brief moment, you shut your mind off and watch the absolute funniest video of a dog driving a car. A wave of serenity washes over you.

“Are you seriously on TikTok right now??”

Dang.. how did she know..

“Do you think I’m dumb or stupid? I can tell by your face that you’re doomscrolling!” she emits.

A woman's intuition really never lies..

She storms away to her room, the video of the dog driving a car is still looping in your head. You sluggishly crawl onto your bed and open your socials. Maybe facing the music will help you get over it faster… but no. The comments are ruthless, people are praying for your downfall, and the trolls are the worst. the kind of comments that are funny until they’re aimed at you. You fall down the rabbit hole of every gossip page, every post, every comment under your hashtag. Then you get an email notification that Dunkin Donuts just dropped you from their brand deal. When will this nightmare end?

Suddenly, you hear the front door slam. Must’ve been your girlfriend leaving. You don’t care, you just continue soaking in the virtual hate. You refresh your homepage on Insta, you see your girlfriend posted a video. The title is “Get ready with me vlog to pack my stuff and leave my boyfriend.”

You delete Instagram. You delete Twitter. Tiktok. All your socials.
Now what.. Where do you scroll now?

You open up Robinhood, just to distract yourself a little. Damn, AMAGOONET stock went down by 15%.
